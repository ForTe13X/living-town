# Coastal reconstruction and character camera

Rebuilt the exterior around limestone pedestrian streets, a civic square, aggregate road zones, garden paths, a coastal promenade and two traversable arcades. The plan contains 11 named routes, 21 entrance links and 916 connected paved cells. Trees and pond outlines were reconstructed; functional destinations and interiors retain their identities.

Nine PixelLab textures and sprites at 256–512 pixels supply terracotta courtyard blocks, a market hall, civic architecture, stone arcades, pines, planters and ground materials. Buildings and trees cast projected alpha shadows; street lamps contribute warm light at night. Asset provenance is in [the coastal kit](../game/assets/art/coastal/README.md).

Life mode follows the interpolated character position with frame-rate-independent smoothing and bounded velocity lookahead. Character selection also starts probe-camera following. Space transitions update camera bounds; reduced-motion mode removes smoothing and lookahead.

The authored plan is generated by `tools/rebuild_coastal_plan.py` and rendered by `CoastalPlan.gd`. `tools/audit_coastal_plan.py` checks material overlap, blockers, connected paving, all 15 functional entrances and both arcade openings.

Validation completed:

- Data lint, map topology audit and coastal-plan audit pass.
- Camera test passes interpolation, frame-rate independence, follow/unfollow and interior transitions; a rendered walk verifies real movement and camera displacement.
- Space and life tests pass. Exterior captures verify nine assets, 50 facades and seven roofs.
- The 60-day simulation run passes the S0 gate across seeds 1–3, including hard checks, social thresholds, liveness and one repeat determinism check. An initial 20-day run was too short for the commitment-lifecycle threshold; it was replaced by the full 60-day validation. Golden digests were not used or rewritten for this change.
- Godot editor retains existing optional-extension / Android-tool / duplicate-UID warnings.
